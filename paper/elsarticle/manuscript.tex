\title{Knowledge-Graph-Augmented and Evidence-Grounded LLM Agents for Risk Information Extraction in Low-Resource Safety Texts: Railway Accident Reports as a Primary Evaluation Scenario}

\begin{abstract}
Safety reports contain sparse but consequential evidence about hazards, failures, controls, events, and causal relations. Extracting this evidence with large language models is difficult when reviewed annotations are limited and outputs must remain traceable to source text. This study proposes an evidence-grounded, knowledge-graph-augmented agent framework for low-resource safety risk information extraction. The framework combines deterministic evidence alignment, ontology-aware retrieval, legal relation-signature constraints, and parameter-efficient adaptation. Railway accident investigation reports form the primary evaluation scenario, while Chinese emergency and safety-management documents are used to test cross-language and cross-domain behavior. The final abstract will report results from an expanded, independently reviewed benchmark; the current 28-document pilot is used only to validate the workflow and identify structured-output failure modes.
\end{abstract}
