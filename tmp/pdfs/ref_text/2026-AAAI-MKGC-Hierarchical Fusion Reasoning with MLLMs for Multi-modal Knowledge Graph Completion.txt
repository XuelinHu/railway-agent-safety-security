Cross-Modal Fusion form the Relation-Guided Hierarchical Modal Fusion module, which performs fine-grained intra-modal
fusion followed by relation-guided weighting for cross-modal integration. (2) MLLMs-Enhanced Knowledge Reasoning
and Scoring: A fine-tuned MLLM generates candidate entities for incomplete triples, whose reasoning outputs are fused with
multi-modal embeddings for final scoring. (3) Multi-modal Negative Sample Optimization: Hard negatives are constructed
via MLLM-based textual perturbation and visual feature augmentation with rotation and noise, and the model is trained using
adversarial learning to enhance robustness and discriminative ability.
